What is implemented

Adaptive space and interval-time similarity, learned MO weights, a small multilingual concept lexicon with TF-IDF/BM25, a Hawkes-inspired interaction, cross-crime compatibility, logistic ranking, and held-out synthetic calibration.

No neural language encoder, police network graph, IPC–BNS mapping, or operational validation is claimed. Source documents remain unchanged.
